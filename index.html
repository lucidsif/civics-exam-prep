    How the test works

    The civics test is oral. The USCIS officer asks up to 10 of the 100 questions. You must answer 6 correctly to pass. You also read one sentence in English and write one sentence in English.

    Important: this app covers the 2008 civics test. USCIS also has a newer 2025 test. Your interview notice and your N-400 filing date decide which test you take — confirm at uscis.gov/citizenship.

    Reading practice

    On test day you read one sentence out loud. Practice with these — every word comes from the official USCIS reading list. Read it out loud, then press “Hear it” to check yourself.

    Writing practice

    The officer says a sentence and you write it. Press play, listen, and type what you hear — every word comes from the official USCIS writing list.

    What to bring

    Your interview appointment notice lists exactly what to bring — follow it. The items below are asked for at every interview:

    • Your interview appointment notice
    • Your Permanent Resident Card (green card)
    • A state ID or driver’s license
    • All passports and travel documents, current and expired
